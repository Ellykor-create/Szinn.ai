Why daily

Recognition is the easy part. The harder part is remembering it on a Tuesday afternoon, in the middle of the situation that keeps repeating. That is what a daily rhythm is for.

You decide the pace. Some people read the morning line and close the app again. Others write every evening. Both are fine, and neither is behind.
