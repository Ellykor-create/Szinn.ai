Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about your Alignment Blueprint

Can't find your question here? Feel free to email us at info@szinn.ai or leave a message on the feedback page.

What is SZINN?

SZINN is a platform for personal growth and self-insight. Based on your birth data, we combine astrology, numerology and Kabbalah into one personal navigation document: the SZINN Alignment Blueprint. SZINN also offers a personal dashboard with a daily reading and an AI Companion that knows your blueprint.

What exactly is the Alignment Blueprint?

Your Alignment Blueprint is an extensive, fully personal document that brings together your birth chart, your numerology life path and your Tikkun (soul correction) from Kabbalah. You receive it as a PDF and as a full personal dashboard in your portal, containing your soul themes, patterns and practical guidance for daily life — plus an AI companion that knows your chart, a daily reading and a journal.

How does the intake work?

After your payment, you fill in the intake form with your name, date of birth, time of birth and place of birth, plus a number of personal questions. Your blueprint is created from these answers. Filling it in takes about 10 to 15 minutes — take your time.

How much does the Alignment Blueprint cost?

The SZINN Alignment Blueprint costs €39.90. This is a one-time payment — not a subscription. You pay securely through Stripe (iDEAL, credit card and more).

When will I receive my blueprint?

You will receive your blueprint within 48 hours — usually much sooner. As soon as it is ready, you get an email with login details for your personal portal, where you can view the interactive version and download the PDF.

What is the SZINN subscription?

Your blueprint comes with a free 11-day trial with access to your personal dashboard. After that, you can continue with the SZINN subscription of €3.69 per month: your daily reading, your journal and 10 SZINN Companion questions per month. The subscription can be cancelled monthly from your dashboard.

How does the SZINN Companion work?

The SZINN Companion is your personal AI conversation partner. It knows your blueprint and birth chart and remembers your conversation, so every session continues where the last one left off. During your trial you can ask 3 questions; with the subscription you have 10 SZINN Companion questions per month.

What do I find on my dashboard?

Your dashboard shows a personal reading every day, based on the current positions of the stars combined with your birth chart. You can also keep your morning start and evening reflection in your journal, talk to the Companion and revisit your blueprint.

What about the privacy of my birth data?

Your birth data is used exclusively to create your personal blueprint and daily reading. We never sell your data and never share it with third parties for commercial purposes. Read all the details in our privacy statement.

Can I cancel my order or get a refund?

The blueprint is a personal, custom-made product that goes into production immediately after your intake. The right of withdrawal therefore lapses once production has started — you confirm this when submitting the intake form. The subscription can be cancelled monthly at any time. Questions? Email info@szinn.ai. See also the terms and conditions.

How do I log in to my account?

After your intake, you will receive the login details for your personal portal by email. You log in at szinn.ai/portaal. Forgot your password? Use the forgot-password option on the login page.

Can I give a blueprint as a gift?

Yes! At szinn.ai/cadeau you can give an Alignment Blueprint as a gift. The recipient receives a personal gift code and then fills in the intake themselves.
