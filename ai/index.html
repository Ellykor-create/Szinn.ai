What the Companion does

It knows your chart

Your planets, houses, numbers and Tikkun themes are the starting point of every answer.

It works with today

Ask about a decision, a conflict or a mood, and it reads the situation against your own pattern.

It does not decide for you

The Companion offers perspective and questions. What you do with it is yours.

How SZINN uses AI

Nothing in your Blueprint is invented by a language model. The astrology is calculated on the Swiss Ephemeris with Placidus houses, the numerology follows the Pythagorean system, and every number is verified in code before it reaches the text.

AI does the writing and the connecting: turning verified calculations and your intake answers into language that reads as one story instead of a table of positions. The method behind it was built and is carried by Elly Elizabeth Korving.

Privacy and your data

Your date, time and place of birth are personal data. They are used to calculate and write your Blueprint and to run the Companion, and for nothing else. They are not sold and not shared with advertisers.

You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by writing to info@szinn.ai. The full privacy statement is at szinn.ai/privacy-en.
