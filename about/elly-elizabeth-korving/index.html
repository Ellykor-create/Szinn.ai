Elly Elizabeth Korving

Founder & Creator · SZINN

Elly is an entrepreneur, business coach and spiritual guide. She works at the intersection of consciousness, strategy and technology, helping people and organisations remember who they are and turn that into a meaningful life and impactful work.

As the founder of SZINN, she brings astrology, numerology, Kabbalah (Tikkun) and sacred geometry together in one system that offers deep insight and practical guidance for everyday life. She is also the founder of 110 Life & Business and co-founder of Alterego, where she has supported entrepreneurs and brands for more than 25 years.

Her work is about bridging the unseen and the practical, so you can live with clarity, purpose and freedom, and create a life that truly feels like you.
