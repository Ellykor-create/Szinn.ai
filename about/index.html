Vision

A world where people live from who they truly are — beyond conditioning, fear, and expectations.

When people remember who they are and live from their own blueprint, they transform not only their own lives, but the world around them.

When enough people remember who they are, the world changes naturally.

The SZINN Alignment Method

SZINN helps you see yourself more clearly. It brings together astrology, Pythagorean numerology and Kabbalah Tikkun to reveal the patterns, qualities and themes that have been part of you from the beginning. Based on your birth details, these perspectives come together in your personal SZINN Alignment Blueprint.

Not to tell you who you should become. Not to predict what will happen. But to help you recognise what is already there.

The name carries three layers: Sin, structure and direction; Sinn, meaning and intuition; Zin, creation and the Dutch word for a life that makes sense.

Recognition → Clarity → Integration

The method guides you through three stages. Because alignment does not begin with becoming someone new. It begins with remembering who you are.
