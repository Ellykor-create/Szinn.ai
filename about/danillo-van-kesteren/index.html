Danillo van Kesteren

Software Developer · SZINN

Danillo is a software developer with a clear mission: to make complex things simple. He uses his technical skills to create digital solutions that help people grow, connect and live with more freedom and awareness.

At SZINN, Danillo builds the platform that translates astrology, numerology and Kabbalah into a personal and practical experience. He combines technical expertise with a deep understanding of the vision, making sure the experience feels intuitive, accessible and meaningful.

Through his company UDefine, he develops innovative web applications and platforms. Danillo believes in the power of technology when it is used with intention: to support human growth, not to replace it.

"I love turning complex ideas into simple, meaningful solutions."
Danillo van Kesteren
