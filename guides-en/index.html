SZINN Guides · SZINN Centers

Someone who stands beside you

Your Blueprint shows you what moves inside you. But reading is not the same as living. A SZINN Guide helps you translate what is written there into your days, your choices, your work. A SZINN Center is the place where you can go for that.

01 · Why

You do not have to figure this out alone

Most people read their Blueprint in one sitting and recognise themselves in it. Then comes the moment where you think: and now what? That is exactly where the work begins. A Guide asks the questions you skip over yourself, and holds the map beside you while you look.

You do not have to prepare anything, and you do not need to know anything about astrology or numbers. Your Guide translates everything into everyday language. You remain the only one who decides what rings true.

◆

You find language for patterns you already felt but never named.

You discover where your energy comes from, and where it drains away.

◆

You turn recognition into something you actually do on Monday.

02 · The difference

Guide or Center?

The difference is mostly in the place and the format. Choose whatever fits you.

A SZINN Guide

A personal guide. You meet one on one, online or in person, and work at your own pace with what your Blueprint holds.

A SZINN Center

A physical place you can walk into. Alongside personal sessions you will find gatherings, groups and other forms of guidance there.

05 · How it goes

From blueprint to conversation

1

Your Blueprint. You start with your own Alignment Blueprint. That is the map the conversation rests on.

2

You choose a Guide. You send a message or call, and together you see whether it clicks.

3

The first conversation. You walk through your Blueprint together and look for where it chafes right now.

4

Onward, at your pace. One conversation is enough if that is enough. If you want to keep going, you can.
